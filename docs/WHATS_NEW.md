# What's new

Newest first. One line per question worked on, new tool or visible change; each question bullet gives its state. Details are on the linked pages; the counts are in the [rating](../README.md#rating).

## 19 November 2026

- **Readme date.** `README.ENG` at file +0x10A is `January 10, 1994`. `lol1_loose.py --check` re-reads those sixteen bytes ([loose files](formats/loose.md)).
- **Cross-series check.** The shared format pages already stated the same archive, movie, music and cache figures as the current notes, and the name-hash note now states the shift of 6 ([engine](engine-evolution.md)). The shared glossary words are the same text in each game ([glossary](GLOSSARY.md)).
- **Settling tests.** Two open statements now name the read that would settle them: the compression-type switch for CPS types 1 and 3, and a search of the wall drawers for the nine piece start offsets ([CPS](formats/cps.md), [walls](formats/vcn_vmp.md)). The view of level 22 block 27 is listed under [probes not yet run](NEEDS_A_RUNNING_GAME.md#probes-not-yet-run).
- **Close-out.** Six open questions (Q1, Q7, Q10, Q14, Q17, Q18) each end with what was measured, what was tried, and what would settle the rest. States stay as they are ([open questions](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)).
- **Readme bytes.** `README.ENG`, beside `DATA` and outside the counted files, holds `LANDS OF` at file +0x72. `lol1_loose.py --check` re-reads those eight bytes ([loose files](formats/loose.md)).
- **Transparency tables.** Table1 of the level files is the twenty colour indexes in `FXPAL.SHP`. The weight-70 nearest-colour mix of `FXPAL.COL` with the KEEP palette matches 3611 of 5120 bytes of `LEVEL01.TLC`; the rule that matches every byte stays open ([transparency tables](formats/tlc.md)).
- **More edited-save runs (Q17 and Q18, both mostly answered).** On a level-8 copy with the lamp lit, the oil ran dry at the instruction the oil rules name, and a walk from level 8 to level 7 was watched; the three isolated save bytes were touched only by the save. A level-27 copy with 500 HP fought the Hatchets under read watches: the critical byte and speed were read, the modifiers and `+0x40` were not. Open: a ranged attack (Q18) and the same events in a game-written save ([edited saves](EDITED_SAVES.md)).
- **Blend tables, third pass.** A self-blend of the paired finale scene palette reproduces 65532 of 65536 bytes of each table: entry `row*256+col` is the nearest colour to `((row*22)>>6) + ((col*42)>>6)` per channel, over the `RETURN.CPS` palette for `BLEND.TAB` and the `FACE.CPS` palette for `BLEND2.TAB`. The last four bytes, `E4 D5 02 00` in both, are not explained and the writing program is still not found, so the six files stay uncounted. `lol1_loose.py --check` now rebuilds both tables ([loose files](formats/loose.md)).
- **Edited saves (Q7, Q17 and Q18, all mostly answered).** Five runs loaded a copy of a game-written save with a few bytes changed by hand: an oil refill and two portraits on level 1, a wall click on level 8, the pose squares on level 23 and a fight on level 27. Each adds evidence to a probe that stays open; [edited saves](EDITED_SAVES.md) lists what each showed and what a game-written save would still add. The editing rules are on the [save page](savegame_format.md#editing-a-copy-of-an-original-slot). Open: wall bit 0x40 and the mode-11 frame (Q7), the oil-depletion writer and a real two-member party (Q17), and the Hatchet fight (Q18).
- **Rating.** The running-game line now also says how many answered probes rest on edited saves only; today none do ([how we rate](HOW_WE_RATE.md)).
- **Picture check.** `example_images.py` also compares the two level schematics in `docs/images` with today's tools.
- **Running-game controls on level 1 (Q7 and Q17, both mostly answered).** The lamp-effect reset from the menu, the walk to the guard and the first audience, and the guard's monster modes were watched on level 1. The three isolated save bytes were touched only by save and load; the guard rows used only modes 1 and 15. Open: any byte-specific use of those three bytes (Q17) and what writes mode 10 (Q7) ([running game](NEEDS_A_RUNNING_GAME.md)).
- **Flag word 15.** The save page said only bit 0x80 of word 0 is read outside the script calls. Word 15 is also read and written directly, at 24 places in the CD program, among them the lamp tests ([save fields](unknowns/17_native_save_fields.md)).
- **Cookbook.** The [cookbook](COOKBOOK.md) gives short recipes on your own game files, each with its command and the first lines it prints.
- **Tools at a glance.** The README lists what you can look at with your own game files, one line and one command each ([tools at a glance](../README.md#tools-at-a-glance)).
- **New inspector tools.** `exe_tables.py` prints the tables inside the game program, `palette_reader.py` prints a palette and draws a swatch, `parse_tim.py --list` prints a cut-scene script command by command, `vcn_vmp_reader.py --walls-png` draws an area's walls as the game draws them, and `level_renderer.py` draws walls in the level's own colours ([tools](../tools/README.md)).
- **Parity table.** [tools/README](../tools/README.md#parity-every-format-and-table-its-reader-and-how-to-look-at-it) lists every format and rules table with its reader, its check and how to look at it. Rows with no such output are marked as gaps.
- **Cut-scene functions (Q6, answered).** The floppy intro and finale TIM files have three function entries, not four ([TIM opcodes](unknowns/06_tim_opcodes.md)).
- **Symbol tables.** The three `FINALE.TDS` files and the three `LANDS.EXE` tails are TDINFO tables. The header counts size every record, the name and index fields stay inside those counts, and only zero bytes sit before the string pool, so those six files are counted as read: 193 of the 199 CD `DATA` files are now read. The six blend tables stay unread ([loose files](formats/loose.md)).
- **Blend tables.** The six files stay unread. No stored 768-byte palette, used whole, keeps the colour on every diagonal entry; the closest (`SCROLL.CPS`, 240 and 233 of 256) leaves a blend over the running game's palette open. The installer status strings still have no caller. Who writes the tables stays open ([loose files](formats/loose.md)).
- **Glossary.** The [glossary](GLOSSARY.md) gives a one-line meaning to each term on the front page, the start page and the cookbook. A word shared with the other two games has the same meaning there. The rating still counts the glossary as a page, but not its label words.
- **Link check.** `doc_links.py` resolves every relative link and anchor in the README and the docs, and `check_all.py` runs it.
- **Twelve loose files, second pass.** The blend tables and the symbol blocks now have a measured layout and a settling test. The six symbol files were later read in full (see **Symbol tables** above); the six blend tables stay unread. None of the eleven simple index formulas tried (minimum, maximum, average rounded down or up, product /255 or >>8, sum, difference, OR, AND, XOR) reproduces a blend table ([loose files](formats/loose.md)).
- **Loose CD files.** `lol1_loose.py` walks 25 files that are not PAK or TLK archives through their last byte; these 25 are among the files counted as read (193 of 199 today). The six not counted are the blend tables; the three `.TDS` symbol files and the three `LANDS.EXE` tails were counted later (see **Symbol tables** above). The page gives the test that would finish each blend table.
- **TIM extra words (Q6, answered).** Nested 0x07 and 0x0D pass those words into the `%` formatter; `lol1_tim_words.py` re-reads the pushes and the strings.
- **Counted rating.** The README and [STATUS](STATUS.md) show four counted lines instead of a percentage; [how we rate](HOW_WE_RATE.md) explains them.
- **Example pictures remade.** The guard is drawn in its real colours, the talking head in its backdrop's palette, and level 1 as a readable map; a check now compares each example with today's tools pixel for pixel ([examples](../examples/README.md)).
- **Quitting the game (Q8, answered).** The answered probe is one quit from play on the English CD with Sound Blaster, traced and re-read in the program. Whether any setup registers a nonzero shutdown callback stays open.
- **Monster fields (Q7 and Q18, both mostly answered).** Both editions' programs were scanned for every reference to the monster skill and modifier fields ([native records](unknowns/18_native_records.md)). Computed aliases to those fields stay open (Q18), as do wall bit 0x40 and one mode-10 producer (Q7).
- **Unused clip and palettes (Q10 mostly answered, Q14 partly answered).** The finale program's file catalog names the silent speech clip, but no code that reaches that row is found. No caller of the three unused palettes is found in any program or the installer. Both stay open until a caller is found.
- **New checks:** `rating.py`, `example_images.py`, `lol1_name_callers.py`; `shp_renderer.py --level` draws monster shapes in their level's palette.
